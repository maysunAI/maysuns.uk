ToDoMemo — Meal & Medication Reminder

A reminder app for families supporting an elderly parent from a distance — live in production

Live

Built for Japan's "遠距離介護" situation — adult children who don't live with an aging parent but still need to know they're eating and taking medication on schedule. Large, simple buttons for meal check-ins with OK/NG color feedback, a straightforward medication list with interval reminders, and real account login so family members can check the same data from their own phones.

Free to use on a single device. Multi-device family sync (see the same records from different phones) is the one feature planned for a paid tier — pricing not finalized yet, still gathering real user feedback first.

→ Try it live at todomemo.maysuns.uk
